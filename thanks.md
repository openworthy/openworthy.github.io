# Thank you

Your licence is on its way by email, usually within a minute. It is a short
piece of text starting with `OWL1.`

To use it, open OpenWorthy, choose **Plan**, then **Add licence**, and paste
it in — or run `openworthy licence add` and paste it.

Your receipt comes separately from Paddle, our reseller; it has the link to
manage or cancel a subscription.

to [{{ site.contact }}](mailto:{{ site.contact }}) and we will send it again.
