# How to flag important emails automatically

Hand-written rules ("if from boss@, flag") break the moment someone new
matters. A better signal is what you already do: who you write to, which
conversations you're in, which mail is addressed to you alone.

OpenWorthy turns exactly those facts into a **Worth Reading** marker:

- **Gmail:** a label, next to (and independent of) Gmail's own Important —
  whether you sign in with Google or use an app password. If you read Gmail
  in Apple Mail or iPhone Mail, which don't show labels, you can choose the
  star instead.
- **Outlook / Microsoft 365:** a category, visible in every Outlook app.
- **iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo, your own server:** the standard flag (a star in
  most apps; green in Apple Mail and iPhone Mail where the server keeps flag
  colours, so it stands apart from your own flags), or a keyword of your
  choice where the server supports keywords.

Because it's an ordinary label, flag or category, it works in your mail app,
on your phone, and in any smart folder or search you build on it.

**Getting started** takes a few minutes: `openworthy account add`, look at
the preview of what it would mark, then `openworthy start`.
