# Pricing

**Free is the whole of OpenWorthy, for one mailbox, with no time limit.**
Pro is for people with more than one mailbox, or who want their mail marked
while their computer is off.

| | Free | Pro |
|---|:-:|:-:|
| Marks the email worth reading, explains every decision, learns from you | ✓ | ✓ |
| Gmail, Microsoft 365 / Outlook.com and any IMAP mailbox | ✓ | ✓ |
| Preview before anything is marked; undo; the daily list | ✓ | ✓ |
| Sensitivity, suggestions and the history of every setting | ✓ | ✓ |
| Desktop app, command line and Docker; any number of your computers | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Mailboxes** | **1** | **Every mailbox you have** |
| **Marking while your computer is off** — your mail server marks mail from the senders OpenWorthy is surest about as it arrives | — | ✓ |
| **Priority email support** | — | ✓ |

Leaving never needs Pro: removing marks, rules, mailboxes and data always
works.

## Pro

time from the link in your receipt.

**What you keep if you stop paying:** Pro keeps working, for good, in every
version released while you paid — including every later fix to those
versions. For example, if your year ends while version 1.4 is current, Pro
keeps working in 1.4 and all its fixes (1.4.1, 1.4.2 …); version 1.5 would
need a renewal. Free keeps working in every version either way, and nothing
is ever deleted or unmarked.

## How it works

- **Buying** takes place in your web browser, through our reseller
  [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com), who handles payment, sales tax and
  invoices. Prices are in US dollars; your local price and any tax are shown
  at checkout.
- **Your licence** is a short piece of text, sent by email. Paste it into the
  app (*Plan → Add licence*). There is no account and no login.
- **It is checked on your computer.** OpenWorthy never contacts us to check
  a licence — which is also why a renewal comes as a new licence to paste in.
- **30-day money-back guarantee.** No questions asked: see the
  [terms](terms.md#refunds).
- **Beta testers** get Pro free until 30 days after version 1.0.
