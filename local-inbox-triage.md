# Local inbox triage

Triage means deciding what needs you now. Doing it locally means that
decision is made on your own device, from data that never leaves it.

OpenWorthy triages locally:

- **Local:** an app on your Mac, Windows or Linux computer — or, if you like,
  on a NAS or Raspberry Pi you own. What it knows stays there, with email
  addresses stored scrambled.
- **Explainable:** every mark says why, in words — "You're in this
  conversation" — not a verdict from a model.
- **Correctable:** remove a mark and it learns; undo a whole batch and it asks
  whether you want fewer marks; every settings change can be reverted.
- **Multi-device aware:** run it on several computers and they coordinate
  through your mailbox so only one marks at a time.
